Privacy Policy

Dino Garden, by Coll Business Solutions LLC. Last updated October 27, 2026.

The short version: Dino Garden has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your child's name, progress and paintings are saved only on your device, and we never receive them.

What Dino Garden keeps, and where

Dino Garden is a game that runs in the web browser on your tablet, phone or computer. To remember where your child left off, it saves a few things in that browser's own storage, on that device only:

  • The child's name, if a grown-up enters one in the settings, so the dino can say it.
  • Progress in the learning games, the marble runs your child built, finished paintings, the pet dino, and settings such as the chosen voice.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We have no server that stores it, and we can't see it.

What Dino Garden doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or email addresses.
  • No advertising, in-app purchases or links that lead a child out of the game.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Things your device and browser do

  • Spoken words. The dino talks using the speech voice built into your device or browser. On most devices that happens on the device itself. Some browsers' online voices, such as Chrome's "Google" voices, send the words being spoken to the browser maker to turn into sound. You can pick a voice in the game's settings.
  • The rounded font. The game's lettering is loaded from Google Fonts the first time the game opens. Like any website request, that shares your device's internet address with Google. After that, the game keeps its own copy and works offline.
  • Hosting. The game's files are served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees the internet address of devices that load them.

Children

Dino Garden is made for young children to play with a grown-up nearby. We don't collect personal information from children, or from anyone. A child's name entered in the settings stays on the device it was typed on.

Removing what's saved

Use Reset all progress in the game's grown-ups settings to clear progress. To remove everything, including the name, delete the Dino Garden icon from the home screen, or clear this website's data in your browser's settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
