What the Academy is, and who it's for

Short, hands-on lessons inside the app. You read a little, then do it yourself in the books of a practice bakery, with a short quiz to check your understanding. Later levels move from the daily work to advising clients and running your own practice.

A good fit if you…

  • want to start working as a bookkeeper for small businesses
  • keep the books for your own business or the office you work in
  • already do bookkeeping and want a structured refresher, with exams that show what you can do

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or 11 computer and the free Coll Ledger app
  • No accounting background for the Professional certificate
  • The Professional certificate before starting the Advanced one
  • About 12½ hours for the first certificate and 8½ for the second, at your own pace

Integrity rules

A certificate is only worth something if people can trust it. These rules are built into the app and the website.

  • Fresh questions each attempt. Every written exam is a new draw from the question bank, covering every topic, with the answers in a new order.
  • A 24-hour wait between retakes. After an attempt that doesn't pass, the next one opens a day later. It's time to review, not to guess again.
  • An honor statement before every exam. You agree, and sign with your name, to work on your own: a calculator and scratch paper are fine, but not notes, the Handbook, other people or AI tools, and you won't share the questions.
  • Credentials verify only after registration. A new certificate is sent to Coll Business Solutions LLC from inside the app. Only after it's registered does its ID show on the verification page.
  • Digitally signed credentials. Every registered credential is signed by Coll Business Solutions LLC with an Ed25519 key, in the Open Badges 3.0 format. The verification page checks the signature in your browser against our published public key, so any change to a name or score shows up. A credential can also be revoked, and then shows as revoked.

What these certificates are not

They are training certificates from Coll Business Solutions LLC that show you completed the Academy and passed its exams. They are not:

  • a CPA license or any other state license
  • an IRS credential, such as Enrolled Agent status or a right to represent taxpayers
  • a credential from a professional accounting body
  • college credit, or credit toward any degree

Recommended next steps

Keep working with clients, and add a recognized credential or an accredited program alongside it.
