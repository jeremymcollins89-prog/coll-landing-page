Brood

Survive · Molt · Leave a brood

A spider survival game. Hatch as one of 12 real spider species, hunt, spin webs and molt your way up from a tiny spiderling, on an island and a backyard garden where you are the top predator. Play alone or online with up to 4 friends.

What you do

Everything is built at spider scale: a fallen leaf is a hill, a blade of grass is a climb, and a sunflower is a skyscraper.

  • 12 real speciesJumping spider, wolf spider, orb weaver, trapdoor, tarantula, black widow, huntsman, diving bell spider, blue baboon tarantula, cat-faced spider, the long-necked pelican spider that hunts other spiders, and one very large surprise.
  • Blue baboon packPlay as a blue baboon tarantula and your own kind travel with you, join your hunts and defend you.
  • Creatures that look and move like the real thingFrogs that sit folded and leap with their legs flung out, birds built feather by feather that flap, glide and stoop, caterpillars that ripple, glistening slugs that feel the air, wriggling mosquito larvae.
  • Xbox controller readyPlug in an Xbox controller on PC and play from the couch: hold LB for the action wheel, hold the D-pad for moods, and every hint switches to the controller's buttons.
  • Hunt your way upStalk flies, crickets, woodlice and caterpillars, build orb webs and trap lines, and pounce. Each species hunts its own way.
  • Fight like the real thingEvery family attacks its own way: jumping spiders leap, wolf spiders pin, tarantulas rear up and stab down, trapdoors burst out of ambush, widows fling sticky silk. Show your mood too: friendly, angry or courting.
  • Music and settingsCalm music by day, quiet at night and a pulse when danger is close. Pick your graphics quality and frame rate, and choose whether death is permanent.
  • Molt and growEat, drink and stay out of trouble to molt from spiderling to adult, then lay an egg sac and carry your lineage on.
  • Two places to liveA leaf-litter island with a pond, reeds and real hazards, and a backyard garden: lawn, sunflowers, tomato cages, clay pots and a compost bin.
  • Online with friendsHost a game from your PC and up to 3 friends can join. Players only: every other spider is a real person. Playing alone, rival spiders, a giant elder and mates share the island with you.
  • Day, night and weatherGolden mornings, cold nights and rain storms change where prey goes and where it is safe to be.

How to install

  1. Click Download for Windows above. Keep the page open until it says it's done (about 20 seconds on a fast connection).
  2. Open Brood-Setup.exe from your browser's downloads (click it at the top right of the browser, or find it in your Downloads folder). If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway (the game is new, so Windows doesn't know it yet).
  3. That's it: Brood installs itself, puts a Brood shortcut on your desktop and in the Start menu, and starts. Next time, just double-click the desktop shortcut.
  4. The first time you host an online game, Windows may ask whether Brood can use your network. Click Allow so friends can join.

Prefer PowerShell?

This one command downloads Brood, installs it, puts the shortcut on your desktop and starts the game. Paste it into PowerShell (Start menu, type PowerShell) and press Enter.

$s="$env:TEMP\Brood-Setup.exe"; $b="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jeremymcollins89-prog/coll-landing-page/main/downloads/brood/setup/0.8.4/Brood-Setup.exe"; $o=[IO.File]::Create($s); foreach($n in 1..10){ $p="$s.part"; Invoke-WebRequest ("$b.{0:D3}" -f $n) -OutFile $p -UseBasicParsing; $i=[IO.File]::OpenRead($p); $i.CopyTo($o); $i.Close(); Remove-Item $p }; $o.Close(); Start-Process $s -ArgumentList '/S'

On your Android phone

  1. On your phone, open this page in Chrome and tap Download for Android. Keep the page open until it says it's done (about a minute on Wi-Fi).
  2. Tap Open on the download message (or open Brood.apk from Files > Downloads).
  3. The first time, Android says Chrome isn't allowed to install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install. If Play Protect asks about an unknown app, tap More details > Install anyway.
  4. Open Brood from your home screen. Turn the phone sideways.

Controls: put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side to move, and push all the way to run. Swipe on the right side to look around. On the right are BITE, JUMP, E (do it), Q (your species' trick) and CREEP; DRINK and WRAP pop up when you can use them. Your spider always grips, so to climb just walk into a stone, leaf or grass blade. Tap MORE for silk, dragline, molt, eggs, moods and the rest. The goals in the corner name the buttons as you go. Online play works on the same Wi-Fi as a PC game.

Playing online

Host a game

  • On the title screen choose Host Online Game.
  • Brood opens the door on your router by itself (UPnP) and shows the address at the top of the screen. Send it to your friends.
  • If it says (your Wi-Fi only), your router didn't let Brood in: friends in your house can still join, and for friends somewhere else turn on UPnP in your router's settings, or forward port 7777 (UDP) to your PC.
  • Your PC is the server, so the game lasts as long as you keep playing. Up to 4 players.

Join a game

  • On the title screen choose Join Online Game.
  • Games on the same Wi-Fi show up by themselves: click Join. For friends somewhere else, type the host's address and choose Connect.
  • Choose Continue to bring the spider you've been growing, or New Game to hatch a new one next to the others.
  • Your spider is saved on your own PC while you play online, so you keep it for next time.
  • Time of day and weather follow the host, so everyone sees the same rain.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no purchases, no account. Brood is in Early Access, which means it's playable now and keeps getting new things.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) with a graphics card that supports DirectX 12, and about 1 GB of free space. A gaming laptop or desktop from the last few years runs it smoothly.

Mac, phone or console?

Brood is on Windows and on Android phones (see On your Android phone; the phone version is brand new, so tell us how it runs). No Mac, iPhone or console yet.

What happens when my spider dies?

By default you go back to your last save (Brood saves every two minutes, and whenever you choose Save Game). If you want the real survival experience, turn on Settings > When You Die > Lose the save: then only an egg sac carries your lineage on. Online, death is always for real, and you hatch again next to your friends.

It runs slowly on my PC

Open Settings and set Graphics Quality to Medium or Low. You can also cap the frame rate there.

My spider got stuck inside something

Brood gets you out by itself: if your spider ends up inside a rock, a log or the ground, it squeezes back out within a moment. You can also open the menu and choose Get Unstuck to go back to the last good spot you stood on.

Where is my save?

Your game saves on your PC, under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Brood\Saved\SaveGames. Deleting the Brood folder doesn't delete it.

The download stopped partway

Click the download button again; it starts over cleanly. Or use the PowerShell command above, which downloads one piece at a time.
