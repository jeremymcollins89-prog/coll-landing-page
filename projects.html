For builders and planners

Coll Plan3D

Turns an AutoCAD drawing (DXF or DWG) or a photo of a plan sheet into a 3D model you can walk through. Lay out modular cleanrooms, warehouses and houses, then hand off a plan sheet and a parts list.

  • Cleanrooms from snap-together panels, doors, pass-thrus, fan filter units and air returns
  • Houses from foundation to roof: several floors, stairs, a garage, kitchens and baths
  • Dimensioned plan sheets, parts lists, and walkthroughs anyone can open in a browser
Download for Windows Phone viewer Windows 10 and 11 · about 90 MB · updates itself

If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. The installer comes from Coll Business Solutions LLC.

For bookkeepers

Coll Ledger

Bookkeeping software for independent bookkeepers who look after several small businesses. Bring in bank, Square and QuickBooks files — it fills in the categories for you to check — and hand each client a polished report.

  • Every client on one screen, with what needs doing at a glance
  • Smart rules and suggestions categorize most bank activity for you
  • Invoices, bills to pay, reconciling, closing the month and client-ready report packages
  • Tax-time ready: trial balance, 1099 contractors, sales tax and a year-end package for the CPA
  • A studio page for her own clients: time tracking and monthly invoices
  • Optional password lock with encrypted books and automatic cloud-folder backups
  • Cash-flow forecast, budgets, mileage log and receipt reading that fills itself in
  • A built-in Bookkeeper's Handbook: tips, rules, deadlines, checklists and FAQs
  • Built-in training class with a practice bakery, quizzes and a certificate

For little kids

Dino Garden

A calm game for toddlers and preschoolers, made for our own three-year-old. A friendly purple dino helps them play, make things and learn at their own pace.

  • Build a marble run, make music, hatch eggs and play matching games
  • Learn colors, shapes, numbers, letters, adding and telling time
  • Paint and color by number, and fly a spaceship around the solar system
Play Dino Garden iPad, iPhone, Android and computers · free · no accounts, no ads · Privacy
Put it on your child's tablet
  1. Open the game in Safari (iPad or iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (or ⋮, then Add to Home screen).
  3. Open Dino Garden from the home screen. It works offline after that.
  4. Tap the settings bubble at the top right to enter your child's name, so the dino can use it.
For grown-ups

Marble Atelier

A calm 3D marble-run game, made at home for our family. Snap glass tubes together, send marbles through loops, portals and chimes, then ride along inside them.

  • Drag and snap tubes, loops, spirals, boosters and portals, at any height
  • Tune lessons teach you to build runs that play songs
  • Color-by-number pictures, including your own photos, and star challenges to find
Play Marble Atelier Made for Android phones · also iPhone and computers · free · no accounts, no ads · everything stays on your phone
Put it on your phone
  1. Open the game in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
  2. Tap ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen (or Share, then Add to Home Screen).
  3. Open Marble Atelier from the home screen once. It works offline after that.
