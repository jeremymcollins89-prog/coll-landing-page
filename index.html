Everything your business needs, in one place

One system for your team, your schedule, your customers, and your bottom line.

Time & attendance

Know who's working, without having to ask.

Real-time overview

See who's clocked in, on break, or off the clock, all in one dashboard.

PIN-based employee login

No passwords or extra accounts for your team to manage — just a simple 4-digit PIN.

Automatic clock-in/out

Employees are clocked in automatically when they arrive at the shop, with manual punches always taking priority.

Break & long-shift alerts

Set each employee's break length and get a "5 minutes left" nudge before it ends. Set a long-shift threshold (1–24 hours) and get a push notification if someone's still clocked in past it.

One-tap payroll

Employees submit their hours, and a formatted timesheet is emailed straight to your payroll inbox.

A little fun at clock-in

Employees can pick their own clock-in celebration — fireworks, a birthday shoutout, or a full rocket launch countdown.

Scheduling & team

Get the right people to the right job.

Job & team scheduling

Build out a shared calendar of jobs, assign a team or individual employees, and everyone gets a push notification and sees it on their phone automatically.

Employees see their schedule

Each employee gets their own calendar showing upcoming jobs, the customer to expect, and directions to the site.

Team chat

Message any employee directly from the admin app — no phone numbers to swap, and it stays with the job.

Customers & billing

From first quote to paid invoice.

Customer directory & history

Keep every customer's contact info in one place, with one-tap calling and directions, plus a full history of their appointments, quotes, and invoices.

Quotes & invoicing

Send a customer a branded quote PDF, then convert it straight into a scheduled job and an invoice once they say yes — with automatic reminders if it goes unpaid.

Get paid online, straight to your bank

Every invoice includes a secure Pay now link for card or bank transfer (ACH). Payments go directly into your own connected Stripe account — not ours — are marked paid automatically, and your customer gets an emailed receipt the moment it goes through.

Money & reports

Know what you actually made.

Expense & profit tracking

Log business expenses, set each employee's hourly pay rate, and see real Gross and Net Profit for any stretch of time — with a full breakdown behind every number.

Profit that accounts for fees

Gross and Net Profit reports factor in real card-processing costs on every online payment, so the number you see is what you actually kept — not just revenue minus labor.

Business reports

See labor hours, invoiced totals, and paid totals for any date range, including how this year stacks up against last year. A dedicated Transactions view breaks every sale down by day, week, month, or year — with payment method and receipt for each one.
