Questions

How much does Coll Timeclock cost?

A 14-day free trial, then $9.99 a month for your business. Cancel anytime.

Do my employees need passwords or their own accounts?

No. Each employee gets a 4-digit PIN and clocks in from the employee app on their phone. There are no passwords or separate accounts for them to manage.

Can employees clock in automatically?

Yes. Set your shop's location and employees are clocked in automatically when they arrive. A manual punch always takes priority.

Does it handle scheduling and routes?

Yes. Build a shared job calendar, assign a team or individual employees, and they get a push notification and see the job, customer and directions on their phone. Multi-stop routes are put in the shortest driving order automatically.

Can I send invoices and get paid online?

Yes. Send branded quotes, turn them into scheduled jobs and invoices, and include a Pay now link for card or bank transfer (ACH). Payments go straight into your own connected Stripe account and invoices are marked paid automatically.

Does it run payroll?

It tracks hours and breaks and emails a formatted timesheet to your payroll inbox. Your payroll provider or accountant pays your team from it.

What do I need to run it?

Nothing to install for employees: they open the clock-in app on their phone. The business owner uses the desktop admin app for Windows or Mac, or the same admin tools in any phone's browser.
