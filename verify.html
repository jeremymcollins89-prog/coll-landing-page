Professional Bookkeeping Certificate

The holder completed all four levels of the Coll Ledger Academy — 29 hands-on lessons covering bookkeeping fundamentals, debits and credits, payroll, journal entries, month-end close and error-finding, cash flow, business types and estimated taxes, and running a professional practice — and passed a written exam (80% or better on 30 questions) and an auto-graded practical month-end close — about 12½ hours of hands-on coursework and exams.

Advanced Accounting & Tax Certificate

Open only to holders of the Professional certificate. The holder completed 14 advanced lessons in three modules — accrual accounting and the close (deferrals, accruals, depreciation, inventory, loans, cash flow), tax compliance (payroll, sales tax, business and owner taxes, 1099s) and professional standards (internal controls, ethics, data security) — and passed an advanced written exam (80% or better on 35 questions) and a graded month-end close with adjusting entries — about 8½ more hours of coursework and exams.

What it isn't

It is a training certificate from Coll Business Solutions LLC. Neither certificate is a CPA license, an IRS credential, a state license, or a credential from a professional accounting body. Questions? Write to collbusinesssolutions@gmail.com.
