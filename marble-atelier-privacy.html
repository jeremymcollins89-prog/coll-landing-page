Privacy Policy

Marble Atelier, by Coll Business Solutions LLC. Last updated November 1, 2026.

The short version: Marble Atelier has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your marble runs, stars, pictures and photos are saved only on your phone, and we never receive them.

What Marble Atelier keeps, and where

To remember your progress, Marble Atelier saves these things in the app's own storage, on your phone only:

  • The marble runs you build, and any copies you keep.
  • Your stars, your place in the tune lessons, and your settings.
  • Your color-by-number pictures and how far you've colored them.
  • Photos you choose to turn into color-by-number pictures. A photo is only read when you pick it, it's kept on the phone, and you can delete it from the picture at any time.

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We have no server that stores it, and we can't see it.

What Marble Atelier doesn't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or email addresses.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or location access. Photos come only from the picker you open yourself.
  • No internet needed to play. Once installed, the game works fully offline.

Backups

You can make a backup file from Menu, Comfort, Make a backup. It's saved to your phone's downloads, and where it goes from there is up to you. We never receive it.

Things your phone and the web do

  • Hosting and updates. The game's files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it sees the internet address of devices that download the game or check for updates.
  • Google Play. If you install Marble Atelier from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own privacy policy. The game itself doesn't send Google anything.

Children

Marble Atelier is a calm game for everyone and isn't directed at children. We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing what's saved

Use Clear the board to empty a run, or remove a photo picture from inside the picture. To remove everything, uninstall the app, or clear this website's data in your browser's settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
