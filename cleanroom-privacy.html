Privacy Policy

Cleanroom Control, by Coll Business Solutions LLC. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Cleanroom Control is a touchscreen controller and online app that companies use to monitor and run their cleanrooms. This policy explains what information the service collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean Coll Business Solutions LLC. "Your company" means the business whose account you use.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Each user's name, email address and role (owner, admin, operator or viewer), and the company they belong to.
  • Passwords, stored only as a one-way hash. We never store or see your actual password.
  • Company settings, such as the company name, time zone and whether signed changes are required.
  • Password-reset and invitation links, which expire.

Information from your controllers

  • Each controller's identity, software version, network status and its address on your local network.
  • The equipment connected to it: device names and types, brand, serial numbers and status.
  • Readings: room pressure, temperature, humidity, particle counts, fan speeds, and light and door states.
  • Room settings: names and areas, alarm limits, schedules and fan modes, pressure control settings, and touchscreen settings.

Records of what happens

  • Alarms: when they were raised and cleared, who acknowledged them, and the note they left.
  • An activity log of changes: who made each change, when, what changed and whether it succeeded. If your company turns on signed changes, the reason given for each change is kept too.
  • Certification records and the files you upload with them, such as certification reports.

Support provider information

If your company adds a support provider, such as the company that installed your cleanroom, we store that link. When someone at your company clicks Request help, we store who asked, when, and the note they wrote.

Phone notifications

If you turn on notifications in the phone app, we store a device token so notifications reach that phone.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising or analytics trackers. The app doesn't use cookies for tracking.
  • No location data from your phone or computer.
  • No camera, microphone or contacts access.

The app keeps your sign-in in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

How we use information

  • To show your rooms' readings live and as history, and to let authorized users run them.
  • To check readings against your alarm limits and send alarm emails to the people your company chooses.
  • To send account emails: invitations, password resets, help requests and the weekly summary, if your company turns it on.
  • To keep the activity log and produce the reports your company runs.
  • To deliver controller software updates when someone at your company starts one.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve it.

Who can see your information

  • Your company. Users at your company see your company's rooms and records, limited by their role.
  • Your support provider. If your company has one, they see only whether each of your rooms is running, down or has an error. They can see and change your rooms only after someone at your company clicks Request help. That access ends on its own after 24 hours, or sooner if an admin ends it. Everything they change is in your activity log under their name.
  • Other companies. They never see your data. Each company's rooms, readings and records are kept separate.
  • Us. We access data only when needed to run, secure or support the service, or when the law requires it.

We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Service providers

A few outside services process data for us, each only to do its job:

  • Railway hosts our servers and database.
  • Resend delivers the emails Cleanroom Control sends.
  • Google Firebase delivers phone notifications, if they are turned on.

These providers may not use your data for their own purposes.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a managed database and sent over encrypted connections. Each controller has its own secret key and can only send and receive its own room's data. Passwords are hashed, and sign-in sessions expire. Each user's role limits what they can change.

How long we keep information

  • Minute-by-minute readings are kept for 35 days. Hourly summaries are kept for as long as your company's account is open, so you keep long-term trends.
  • Alarms, the activity log and certification records are kept while your account is open, because they are often needed for audits.
  • Account information is kept until the user or company account is deleted.

Your choices and requests

Admins can edit and remove users, rooms, alarm limits and certification files in the app. Any user can change their name, email address and password on the Account page.

To ask for a copy of your data, or to delete a user account or your company's whole account, email collbusinesssolutions@gmail.com from the address on the account. We'll confirm by email once it's done. Some records may be kept longer if the law requires it, and we'll tell you if that applies.

Children

Cleanroom Control is a workplace tool and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date. For important changes, we'll also let account owners know by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: collbusinesssolutions@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
