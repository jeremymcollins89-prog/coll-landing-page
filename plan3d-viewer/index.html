Getting a file onto your phone
  1. In Coll Plan3D on your computer, save the plan (File → Save).
  2. Send the .plan3d file to yourself: email, text, AirDrop, or a Google Drive or OneDrive folder.
  3. On the phone, save it to Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android).
  4. Tap Open a Plan3D file above and pick it.
Put the viewer on your home screen
  1. iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).
