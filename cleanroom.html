Everything your cleanroom needs, in one system.

Monitoring and alarms

Know the moment something changes.

  • Live readings Pressure, temperature, humidity and particle counts, live on the wall screen and in the app.
  • Alarm emails Set limits for each reading. Cross one and the right people get an email, and a horn or strobe can sound in the room.
  • History and trends Every reading is recorded, even through an internet outage, with trend charts and a weekly summary email.

Control

Run the room from one place.

  • Fan filter units Set each fan's speed or all of them at once. Fans keep their speed even if the controller restarts.
  • Automatic pressure Set a pressure target and the controller adjusts the fans to hold it, through doors opening and filters loading.
  • Schedules Full speed during production, a lower setback speed nights and weekends.
  • Areas within a room Name the spaces one controller runs, and alarms say exactly where.
  • Day and night looks The wall screen can use a light look, a dark look, or Day/night, which switches by itself: light during working hours and dark at night. Change it on the screen or from the app.

Access and records

Who changed what, and when.

  • Roles for your team Owners, admins, operators and viewers each see and change only what they should. The wall screen can be locked with a PIN.
  • Activity log and signed changes Every change is logged with who made it. Turn on signed changes to require a password and a reason for each one.
  • Reports Readings, alarms and changes for any date range, ready for audits and certification.

Help when you need it

Your installer is one button away.

  • Request help Your installer sees whether your rooms are running. Click Request help and they can look in and fix things for 24 hours, then access closes on its own.
  • Updates over the internet New controller software installs from the app in about a minute. If anything goes wrong, the controller goes back to its previous version by itself.
  • Private by design Each company's rooms, readings and records are kept separate from every other company.

Your room, from anywhere.

Check on your cleanroom and change it from any computer or phone, wherever you are: the office, home, or another site across the country. Sign in from a web browser and you see the same live room the wall screen shows.

  • Any computer or phoneWindows, Mac, iPhone or Android. If it has a web browser, it can run your room.
  • Every room on one screenAll your rooms and sites side by side, with live readings and whether each one is running normally.
  • Change it, not just watch itFan speeds, the pressure target, schedules and alarm limits can all be changed from wherever you are.
  • Alarms that find youAn email the moment a reading leaves its range, so you know before anyone calls.
  • No VPN or network setupPlug the controller into the internet and it connects on its own. Nothing to open on your firewall.
  • Keeps working offlineIf the internet drops, the wall screen keeps running the room and checking limits, and readings are kept until it's back.

Works with your equipment.

Connects to the fan filter units and sensors cleanrooms already use. Not sure about yours? Send us the model and we'll tell you.

Fan filter units

  • Envirco, through its Universal Control Card
  • Terra Universal ECM
  • AJ Manufacturing Unit Manager
  • ebm-papst EC motors
  • Other brands with a Modbus port, such as CRI, Price and Nortek, added from the maker's register list
  • Any fan with a 0–10 V speed input, through a Cleanroom Control fan module

Room pressure

  • Sensirion SDP810 differential pressure sensors, for room-to-room readings
  • Any room pressure transmitter with a Modbus RS-485 output

Temperature and humidity

  • Sensirion SHT40, SHT41 and SHT45 sensors
  • XY-MD02 / SHT20 and RS-WS Modbus transmitters
  • Other Modbus transmitters, added from the maker's register list

Particle counters

  • Plantower PMS5003 sensors, for particle trends and alarms
  • Modbus particle counters such as Lighthouse and Met One, for certification-grade monitoring

Lights, doors and alarms

  • Room lights, switched through a relay module
  • Door switches, to show when a door is open
  • A horn or strobe light that sounds during an alarm

Building systems and network

  • Building management systems, over Modbus TCP
  • Ethernet, with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi as a backup
