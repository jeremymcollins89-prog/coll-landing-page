One controller per room. Everything else happens in the app.

1

Install the controller

Mount the touchscreen and connect your fan filter units and sensors. Each one is found and named on its own — FFU-1, FFU-2, TH-1 — with no programming.

2

Pair it to your account

Connect it to your network and the screen shows a pairing code. Enter the code in the app and the room appears on your account.

3

Run it from anywhere

Watch every reading, change fan speeds and limits, and get alarm emails — from the wall, your desk or your phone.

Everything your cleanroom needs, in one system.

Monitoring and alarms

Know the moment something changes.

Live readings

Pressure, temperature, humidity and particle counts, updated live on the wall screen and in the app.

Alarm emails

Set limits for each reading. Cross one and the right people get an email, and a horn or strobe can sound in the room.

History and trends

Every reading is recorded, even through an internet outage, with trend charts and a weekly summary email.

Control

Run the room from one place.

Fan filter units

Set each fan's speed or all of them at once. Fans keep their speed even if the controller restarts.

Automatic pressure

Set a pressure target and the controller adjusts the fans to hold it, through doors opening and filters loading.

Schedules

Run full speed during production and a lower setback speed nights and weekends, automatically.

Areas within a room

Name the spaces one controller runs — gowning, airlock, fill room — and alarms say exactly where.

Access and records

Who changed what, and when.

Roles for your team

Owners, admins, operators and viewers each see and change only what they should. The wall screen can be locked with a PIN.

Activity log and signed changes

Every change is logged with who made it. Turn on signed changes to require a password and a reason for each one.

Reports

Readings, alarms and changes for any date range, ready for audits and certification.

Help when you need it

Your installer is one button away.

Request help

Your installer sees whether your rooms are running. Click Request help and they can look in and fix things for 24 hours, then access closes on its own.

Updates over the internet

New controller software installs from the app in about a minute. If anything goes wrong, the controller goes back to its previous version by itself.

Private by design

Each company's rooms, readings and records are kept separate from every other company on Cleanroom Control.
