Privacy policy

Coll Business Solutions LLC · Colorado · Last updated October 2026

This policy covers the Coll Ledger Academy, the Coll Ledger desktop app for Windows, and the Coll Ledger Academy phone app at collbusinesssolutions.com/academy-app, all from Coll Business Solutions LLC ("we", "us"). It explains what we keep, why, and how to have it deleted. See also our terms of use.

The short version

  • Lessons and practice clients stay on your device.
  • Real client books in the desktop app never leave your computer.
  • For official exams we keep your name, email, exam results and credentials.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics cookies, and we never sell your data.
  • Ask us any time to delete your data.

1What we collect, and why

You can do every lesson without giving us anything. We only collect information when you sign up for official final exams, which are graded on our server. Here is everything the server keeps:

Your nameTo print on your certificate and show on the verify page.
Your emailTo reach you about your exams and credentials, and to confirm it's you when you ask for a change or deletion. We don't verify it or send marketing.
A hashed sign-in tokenSo you can sign in with your student code. We store a scrambled (hashed) form, not the code itself.
Exam attempts and scoresTo grade exams, enforce the retake wait, and decide whether to issue a certificate.
Practical-exam submissionsThe practice-company books you hand in, so they can be graded. These contain practice data only, never real clients.
Synced Academy progressWhich lessons you've finished, so your progress follows you between devices and counts toward exam eligibility.
Issued credentialsYour name, the certificate, your scores and the dates, so anyone you share your credential ID with can check it.

Like any website, the services that deliver our pages and server (see section 3) briefly process technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver them and guard against abuse. We don't use that to track you.

2What stays on your device

  • Lessons and practice clients. In the phone app they live in your browser's storage; in the desktop app, in a file on your computer. Clearing your browser data or deleting the file removes them.
  • Real client books. Books you keep for real clients in the Coll Ledger desktop app never leave your computer. We never see them.
  • The apps keep a few settings (like light or dark mode) on your device too. These are not tracking cookies.

3Where it's stored

  • Exam server: Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare storage, in the United States.
  • This website and the phone app: hosted on GitHub Pages. The Windows installer is downloaded from GitHub.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it sends them.

These companies act as our service providers and have their own privacy policies.

4What's public

When we issue a credential, anyone who has its credential ID can look it up on our verify page. They will see your name, the certificate, your scores and the dates, and whether it is valid or revoked. They can also download the signed credential file (an Open Badges 3.0–style file), which contains the same details.

Your email, student code and exam answers are never shown publicly. We don't publish a list of holders; someone needs your credential ID to look you up.

5How long we keep it

  • Your account, progress and exam records are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Issued credentials are kept so employers and others can keep verifying them, unless you ask us to delete them or we revoke them. A revoked credential may stay listed as revoked so people can't rely on an old copy.
  • We may keep a small record that a deletion or revocation happened, and anything the law requires us to keep.

6Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • with the service providers above, only to run the Academy;
  • through the verify page, for credential IDs you choose to share;
  • when the law requires it, such as a valid court order, or to protect people's safety or our rights;
  • with a new owner if Coll Business Solutions LLC or the Academy is ever sold or transferred, under this same policy.

7Security

Connections to our site and server are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored hashed, and credentials are digitally signed so they can't be altered without detection. We collect as little as we can. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your student code private and tell us if you think it has been exposed.

8Children

The Academy is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to use it. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

9Your choices

  • Delete your data. Ask us to delete your account, progress, exam records and credentials. Email us from the address you signed up with, or use the delete option in the Academy where it's offered. Once deleted, your credentials will no longer verify.
  • Correct your name. Email us and we'll fix it and reissue your credential.
  • Download your credential. Get your signed credential file any time from the verify page.
  • Use it offline. You can skip the official exams and use every lesson without giving us any information.

We'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your data; we'll honor them.

10Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll tell you in the Academy or by email before they take effect.
